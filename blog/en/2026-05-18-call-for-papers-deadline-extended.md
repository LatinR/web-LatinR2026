<h3><center>CALL FOR PAPERS — DEADLINE EXTENDED</center></h3>

<br><br>

We have great news! We have decided to extend the submission deadline for LatinR 2026.

<div class="boxBorder">
<h4 style="text-align:center;"><strong>New deadline: June 1, 2026</strong></h4>

Submit your proposal via [**OpenReview**](https://openreview.net/group?id=LATIN-R.com/2026/Conference) by **June 1, 2026**.
</div>

<br>

If you haven't submitted your work yet, this is your chance! You can participate with oral presentations, lightning talks, or posters.

### How to submit your work

1. Register on [OpenReview](https://openreview.net/group?id=LATIN-R.com/2026/Conference) if you don't have an account yet. Please note that **account creation can take up to two weeks** for personal (non-institutional) accounts. Don't leave it to the last minute!
2. Prepare your abstract in PDF format, without author names or affiliations, in letter size, font size 11 with single spacing, no longer than two pages.
3. Submit your proposal before **June 1, 2026** through the [OpenReview](https://openreview.net/group?id=LATIN-R.com/2026/Conference) platform.

<br>

#### Updated important dates

**Start of work submission**: Mid April

**New deadline for proposal submission**: **June 1, 2026**

**Notification of evaluation results**: late July

**Deadline for confirming participation**: early August

**Conference dates**: November 11–13

<br>

We look forward to receiving your proposal!
