<h3><center>CALL FOR PAPERS — DEADLINE EXTENDED AGAIN</center></h3>

<br><br>

We have great news! We have decided to extend the submission deadline for LatinR 2026 once more.

<div class="boxBorder">
<h4 style="text-align:center;"><strong>New deadline: June 15, 2026</strong></h4>

Submit your proposal via [**OpenReview**](https://openreview.net/group?id=LATIN-R.com/2026/Conference) by **June 15, 2026**.
</div>

<br>

If you haven't submitted your work yet, this is your last chance! You can participate with oral presentations, lightning talks, or posters.

### Submission types

- **[Oral presentations](https://openreview.net/group?id=LATIN-R.com/2026/Conference)**: 15-minute talks (plus 5 minutes for questions) on research results, progress reports, or developments with impact on the use or development of R in academia or industry.

- **[Lightning talks](https://openreview.net/group?id=LATIN-R.com/2026/Conference)**: 5-minute presentations to share advancements, ideas, ongoing research, experiences, and challenges related to LatinR's themes.

- **[Posters](https://openreview.net/group?id=LATIN-R.com/2026/Conference)**: an opportunity to present ongoing projects and progress during a dedicated session for dialogue with the audience.

<br>

### How to create an OpenReview account

If you don't have an OpenReview account yet, follow these steps:

1. Go to [openreview.net](https://openreview.net) and click **"Sign Up"**.
2. Fill in the form with your first name, last name, and email address.
   - If you use an **institutional email** (university or recognized organization), your account will be activated within a few days.
   - If you use a **personal email** (Gmail, Hotmail, etc.), the verification process can take **up to two weeks**. Don't leave it to the last minute!
3. Check your inbox and follow the confirmation link.
4. Once your account is active, go to the conference platform: [LATIN-R.com/2026/Conference](https://openreview.net/group?id=LATIN-R.com/2026/Conference).

<br>

### How to submit your work

1. **Create your OpenReview account** in advance (see instructions above).
2. **Prepare your abstract** in PDF format, without author names or affiliations, in letter size, font size 11 with single spacing, no longer than two pages. You may use [this template](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1KrPbi2AR5Rcq5fKMkC_yK_9gpez4Fmtz/edit?usp=sharing&ouid=107644076848762167027&rtpof=true&sd=true) if you prefer a word processor.
3. **Submit your proposal** before **June 15, 2026** through the [OpenReview](https://openreview.net/group?id=LATIN-R.com/2026/Conference) platform.
