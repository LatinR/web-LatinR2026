Keynote Speakers

In this ninth edition, we will have the presence of prominent speakers in the field of programming with R, with experience in both academia and industry, committed to collaboration and the promotion of open science, data, and software.

Speakers

Nic Crane

Nic Crane is an open-source maintainer and independent R consultant and educator. They are a member of the Apache Arrow Project Management Committee and one of the maintainers of the Arrow R package, contributing to the broader Arrow ecosystem and its adoption across data science workflows. Nic is a contributor to the ellmer R package and teaches and writes about using LLMs in R (https://niccrane.com/).
